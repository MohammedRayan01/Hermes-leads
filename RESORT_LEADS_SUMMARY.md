## 🔍 Data Quality Assurance

Each lead in the **no-website list** went through **3-layer verification**:

1. **Feed-text detection** — checked Google Business Profile text for URLs
2. **Place-page revisit** — verified against the authoritative Maps listing
3. **Live web search** — DuckDuckGo cross-check to catch businesses with websites not linked on their profile

**Excluded from lists:**
- Businesses with only social media (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)
- Businesses with only directory links (Booking.com, etc.)
- Businesses missing a phone number (not contactable)
- Duplicate businesses across multiple searches
