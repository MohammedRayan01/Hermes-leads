### Column Reference

### Sample Call Script (No-Website Leads)
> "Hi [Resort Name]! I found your resort on Google Maps—I can see you have [X] reviews and a [Y] star rating, which is great. I help resorts get their own website so guests can book directly instead of only finding you on Google or Booking.com. Do you currently have a website?"

### Best Practices
1. **Sort by rating** — Start with 4.5+ star resorts (quality prospects)
2. **Filter by review count** — Higher reviews = busier resorts = more booking revenue to invest
3. **Call during business hours** — 10 AM–1 PM or 3 PM–6 PM (avoid meals)
4. **Weekdays > weekends** — Better availability on Mon–Fri
5. **Have a CTA ready** — "Can we schedule 15 minutes next week to walk you through our website demo?"
